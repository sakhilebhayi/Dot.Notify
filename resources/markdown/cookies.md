This Cookie Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technology on Dot.Notify.

## 1. What cookies we use

Dot.Notify uses one essential cookie, `dot-notify-session`, to keep you signed in and to protect your session against cross-site request forgery. This cookie is strictly necessary for Dot.Notify to work — without it, you'd need to sign in again on every page.

We don't use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## 2. Tracking pixels and link tracking in sent notifications

Separately from cookies, notifications sent through Dot.Notify on a team's behalf may include a tracking pixel (to record when an email is opened) or tracked links (to record when a link is clicked). This is not a cookie — it's how delivery/engagement status (§4 of our Privacy Policy) is measured for the team that sent the notification. If you have questions about tracking in a specific notification you received, contact the team or platform that sent it.

## 3. Why we use the session cookie

The `dot-notify-session` cookie lets Dot.Notify recognise you as you move between pages after signing in, and helps protect the authenticity of requests you submit (creating channels, templates, rules, or webhooks).

## 4. How long it lasts

The session cookie lasts for the duration of your browsing session, or longer if you select "Remember me" when signing in, per your browser's session-cookie handling.

## 5. Managing cookies

Because this cookie is essential to signing in and using Dot.Notify, there's no in-app way to disable it while remaining signed in. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying signed in to Dot.Notify.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
